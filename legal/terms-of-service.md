## 2. What GigAway is, and what it is not

GigAway is a directory and introduction service for professional performing
artists. It lets members find each other by city and date, and arrange informal
hospitality between themselves.

**GigAway is not an accommodation provider.** Read this section carefully,
because it defines the limits of what we do.

- **No money changes hands in the app.** There is no payment system, no deposit,
  no fee, and no commission. GigAway earns nothing from any stay.
- **GigAway is not a party to any arrangement between members.** When you agree
  to host or to stay, that agreement is between the two of you. We are not a
  landlord, agent, broker, insurer or guarantor.
- **We do not inspect homes.** We have never seen any member's home and make no
  representation about whether it is safe, clean, legal or as described.
- **We do not vouch for members.** Verification confirms professional standing
  as an artist, and that the name on the account matches a selfie taken
  holding photo ID. It is not a formal identity check, a background check, a
  criminal record check, or a statement that someone is safe to stay with.

## 4. Verification

Every application is reviewed by a human: a selfie of you holding photo ID,
taken against a pose we specify at the time so it cannot be an old or reused
photo; the full legal name on that ID; and evidence of your professional
standing, such as a CV or links to your portfolio, projects, video work or
social profiles. We may approve or reject any application at our discretion,
and we are not obliged to explain a rejection in detail — a rejected
application may be submitted again.

The selfie, the ID and the CV are retained for as long as your account exists
— not deleted once a decision is made, and not on a fixed schedule — so a
decision can be revisited and the fact that a real human was checked stays
part of your record. See Section 7 of the [Privacy Policy](./privacy-policy.md)
for the full detail.

## 5. Your responsibilities when you host or stay

This is the section that matters most.

**You are responsible for your own safety.** Read profiles and reviews, ask
questions, and do not agree to anything you are not comfortable with. You can
withdraw from any arrangement at any time, for any reason, including after
agreeing to it. Nothing in GigAway obliges you to go through with a stay.

**You are responsible for your own legal position.** Hosting a guest may require
your landlord's permission, may affect your tenancy, and may have tax or
registration consequences where you live. Some leases prohibit guests. Some
cities require registration of visitors. Checking this is your responsibility,
not ours.

**You are responsible for your own insurance.** GigAway provides no insurance of
any kind. Your home contents insurance may not cover guests, and your travel
insurance may not cover an informal stay. Check before you agree.

**Be honest.** Describe what you are actually offering. If your "couch" is a
mattress in a shared kitchen, say so. Misrepresentation is grounds for removal.

**Turn up, or say you cannot.** Cancelling late leaves someone without a bed in a
city they do not know. If plans change, say so as early as you can.

## 8. Reviews

After a stay, both parties may review each other. Reviews are attributed to you
by name and published double-blind — neither is shown until both are submitted,
or 14 days have passed.

Write honestly and about your own experience. We may remove reviews that breach
the community guidelines, contain personal data about third parties, or are
retaliatory rather than descriptive. We do not remove reviews for being
unflattering.

## 9. Reporting, and what we do about it

You can report any member from their profile or from a stay.

**Reports are confidential.** The reported person is never shown the report, and
never told who made it.

**We aim to review every report within 24 hours.** Depending on what we find, we
may take no action, contact those involved, issue a warning, suspend an account
pending investigation, or remove a member permanently.

**Suspension takes effect immediately.** A suspended account loses access to all
member content at once.

Reports are retained even if the reported member deletes their account, so that
deleting and rejoining cannot clear a safety record.

**If you are in danger, contact the emergency services first.** GigAway is not an
emergency service and cannot intervene in a live situation. In the EU, call 112.
