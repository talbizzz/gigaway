# Community Guidelines

**Last updated:** 2 September 2026

GigAway works because it is small, verified, and made of people who share a
profession. Everyone here is a colleague. These are the expectations that keep
it that way.

---

## The short version

**Be the guest you would want in your own home. Be the host you would want in a
strange city.**

---

## If you are hosting

**Describe what you actually have.** A couch is a couch. If it is a mattress in
the kitchen, or a room you share with a flatmate, say so. Somebody is planning
their trip around your description, and finding out at 11pm that it was
optimistic is genuinely bad.

**Say what your household needs.** No smoking, no pets, quiet after ten, women
only, no children — set these when you post your availability. Constraints are
not rudeness. They prevent bad matches.

**Be reachable in the days before.** Once contact details are exchanged, answer
messages. Your guest is travelling to a city they may not know.

**Offering a couch is not offering yourself.** Hospitality is hospitality.
Nothing else is implied by it, ever, in either direction.

**You can change your mind.** If something feels wrong, say no — before or after
agreeing. Do it as early as you can, but do it. Nobody is owed a bed.

## If you are staying

**Turn up when you said, and leave when you said.** Your host has arranged their
week around you.

**Tell them as soon as plans change.** Auditions move and trains are cancelled.
Silence is the problem, not the change.

**Treat the home as someone's actual home.** It is not a hotel. There is nobody
cleaning up behind you.

**Respect the house rules**, including the ones you find unnecessary.

**Do not bring anyone who was not agreed.** Your host said yes to you.

**Say thank you.** Nobody is being paid for this.

## Everyone

**No money.** GigAway is free and stays free. Do not ask for payment, offer
payment, or use it to run a commercial letting. Splitting a hotel room you both
booked is fine — that is what co-accommodation is for.

**Keep contact details private.** When someone's email and WhatsApp are revealed
to you, that is for arranging your stay. Not for adding them to anything, not
for passing on, not for anything else.

**Be honest about who you are.** Misrepresenting your professional standing
undermines the one thing that makes this community work.

**Do not photograph the inside of someone's home** without asking. Do not post
photographs of it anywhere.

**Write reviews about the experience, not the person.** "Cancelled the night
before" is useful. Insults are not. Reviews are attributed to you, and shown to
both sides at once.

## What gets you removed

Some of these end an account immediately and permanently:

- **Any form of sexual pressure, harassment or unwanted advance**
- **Threats, intimidation or violence**
- **Discrimination** on grounds of race, nationality, religion, gender, sexual
  orientation, disability or age
- **Entering someone's home under false pretences**
- **Theft or deliberate damage**
- **Sharing another member's contact details, photos or messages**

Others get a warning first, and removal if repeated:

- Repeatedly cancelling at the last minute or failing to appear
- Misrepresenting what you are offering, or who you are
- Commercial use — charging, advertising, recruiting or selling
- Persistent unwanted contact after someone has stopped replying

## Reporting someone

Every profile has a report option, and so does every stay.

**Reports are private.** The person you report is never shown your report and is
never told who made it. This is not negotiable — it is what makes reporting
safe enough to be worth doing.

**We aim to look at every report within 24 hours.** We may take no action,
contact the people involved, issue a warning, suspend an account while we look,
or remove someone permanently. Suspension takes effect immediately.

**Blocking is separate, and instant.** Blocking someone makes you invisible to
each other straight away. You do not need a reason, and you do not need to
report them as well.

**If you are in immediate danger, call the emergency services first.** In the EU
that is **112**. GigAway cannot intervene in a live situation. Report to us
afterwards so we can act on the account.

## A note on trust

Verification means somebody read an application and confirmed the person is a
working artist. **It is not a background check, an identity check, or a promise
that a person is safe.**

Read profiles. Read reviews. Ask questions before you agree. Tell a friend where
you are staying. Trust your own judgement over a green tick — and if something
feels wrong, leave. Nothing here obliges you to stay anywhere.
