# Deleting your GigAway account

This page explains how to delete your **GigAway** account, and exactly what
happens to your data when you do.

GigAway is operated by Mohamed Aziz Talbi. If you have any question about
deletion, write to `privacy@gigaway.app`.

---

## How to delete your account

**In the app — the fastest route:**

1. Open **GigAway**
2. Go to the **Settings** tab
3. Tap **Delete account**
4. Confirm

Deletion happens immediately. You will be signed out and cannot sign in again.

**If you no longer have the app installed**, or cannot sign in, email
`privacy@gigaway.app` from the address on your account and ask for it to be
deleted. We complete the request within 30 days, and usually much sooner.

---

## Deletion is permanent

There is no undo and no grace period. The account cannot be restored, and
signing up again later creates a new account with no connection to the old one.

---

## What is permanently erased

- Your login and password
- Your email address, phone number and WhatsApp number
- Your profile photo, biography, specialisation, home city and district, and
  professional links
- Your verification application record, **including the selfie, ID photo and
  CV you submitted with it** — these are kept for as long as your account
  exists specifically so this is the point they go; see the Privacy Policy,
  section 3.3
- Push notification tokens and your notification history
- Contact-sharing grants, so nobody keeps access to your details
- Blocks you created
- Reviews other people wrote **about** you
- Reviews you wrote that had not yet been published
- Pending requests and offers
- Your availability
- Trips that never led to a stay

## What is kept, no longer linked to you

Your profile becomes an anonymised record showing **"Deleted member"**. The
following survive and point at that record rather than at you:

- **Stays you took part in** — dates and city. This is the other person's record
  of where they hosted, or were hosted
- **Published reviews you wrote about other people**, shown as written by
  "Deleted member"
- **Reports**, in both directions
- **Blocks other people made against you**
- Trips that produced a stay, reduced to dates and a city with your notes removed

### Why these are kept

Three of them are deliberate, and worth being direct about.

**Reports** are kept because otherwise anyone could clear their safety record by
deleting and rejoining.

**Published reviews you wrote** are kept because otherwise deleting and rejoining
would erase the reputation record of the people you stayed with.

**Blocks made against you** are kept because someone who blocked you for a reason
should not be silently re-exposed to you if you return.

The legal basis is our legitimate interest, and the legitimate interests of other
members, in a community where safety records cannot be erased by leaving. You may
object under Article 21 GDPR by writing to `privacy@gigaway.app`.

---

## Retention period

Everything in the "permanently erased" list is deleted **immediately**, not after
a delay. There is no backup retention window during which your account could be
restored.

Anonymised records are kept for as long as GigAway operates, because they belong
to other members' histories rather than to yours.

---

## Getting a copy first

Deletion is irreversible, so export your data before you delete if you want a
copy. **Settings → Export my data** produces a machine-readable file.

The export excludes reports in both directions, and reviews still inside the
14-day double-blind window. Returning them would reveal who reported whom, or let
the export be used to see a review before it is published.
