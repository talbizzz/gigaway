A couch, a colleague, a city you don't know yet.

GigAway is a verified network for professional performing artists who travel for auditions, competitions and guest contracts. Log a trip, and find verified colleagues in that city offering a free couch, local knowledge, or company.

No money changes hands. GigAway is not an accommodation provider and takes no fee.
